## About me

I've spent 5+ years delivering software for clients in Australia, the US, and Nigeria. That work covers government and development-finance programmes, banking-grade messaging infrastructure, education platforms, and a lot of e-commerce.

What makes me different is that I don't hand off at the edge of the spreadsheet. On most projects I'm the TPM, the QA lead, and when it's needed, the developer as well. I scope the work, run the sprints, read the pull requests, write the tests, deploy the system, and manage the client relationship. Doing all of that has made me allergic to clever solutions nobody can maintain six months later.

Today I'm **TPM & Product Lead at [Visionary Insights](https://visionaryinsights.co)**, a US-based AI-native venture studio, and I run client delivery through my own practice.
